# MAN 1.0.6

## 新功能

- 篮球27「街球之王」场地正式上线。1.0.5 曾误随包出现，本次为正式推出。
- 键盘接管时会暂停识别，松开后自动恢复。

## 修复

- 优化场地定位、跑位与投篮校准。
- 提升菜单、赛果与重连识别。
- 修复街球配置影响开放球场的问题。
- 更新 VC 与时薪统计，改善 Xbox 中继与失败反馈。

## New features

- Basketball 27 Streetball King court is now officially available. It was unintentionally included in 1.0.5; this is its formal launch.
- Keyboard takeover pauses analysis and resumes it after the keys are released.

## Fixes

- Improved court positioning, movement, and shooting calibration.
- Improved menu, result, and reconnection recognition.
- Prevented Streetball settings from affecting Open Court.
- Updated VC and hourly-rate reporting, Xbox relay, and failure feedback.
